Wireless Mesh DAQ — Solar Telemetry and Fault Interpretation Guide

Purpose
This document describes the telemetry and fault states that the live Wireless Mesh DAQ demo currently supports. The assistant must distinguish raw measurements from interpreted fault states and must not claim unsupported diagnoses.

Live telemetry fields
- voltage: panel voltage in volts (V)
- current: panel current in amperes (A)
- power: measured panel power in watts (W)
- temperature: simulated panel temperature in degrees Celsius (°C)
- irradiance: simulated plane-of-array solar irradiance in watts per square meter (W/m²)
- expected_power: irradiance- and temperature-adjusted expected panel power in watts
- performance_ratio: measured power divided by expected power
- environmental_state: either low_irradiance or productive_irradiance
- diagnostic_basis: the deterministic reason associated with the current status

Nominal reference
At standard productive conditions, the simulated panel is approximately 39 V and 7.5 A, with nominal rated power of 292.5 W. Expected power is adjusted for irradiance and panel temperature. Higher panel temperature reduces expected power using a demonstration coefficient of approximately -0.4% per °C above 25 °C.

Supported status categories, in evaluation order

1. LOW_IRRADIANCE — informational
Trigger: irradiance < 100 W/m².
Meaning: sunlight is too low for meaningful electrical fault diagnosis. Low output under this condition is not treated as a panel fault.

2. DEAD_PANEL — critical
Trigger: irradiance >= 400 W/m², voltage <= 1.5 V, and current <= 0.15 A.
Meaning: the panel is producing neither useful voltage nor current despite adequate sunlight.

3. SHORT_CIRCUIT — critical
Trigger: irradiance >= 400 W/m², voltage <= 2.0 V, and current >= 5.0 A.
Meaning: voltage has collapsed while current remains abnormally high.

4. OPEN_CIRCUIT — high
Trigger: irradiance >= 400 W/m², voltage >= 25 V, current <= 0.15 A, and power <= the greater of 5 W or 2% of expected power.
Meaning: voltage is present but current and power are near zero despite adequate sunlight.

5. OVER_TEMPERATURE — warning
Trigger: panel temperature >= 70 °C.
Meaning: panel temperature is above the demo warning threshold. This threshold is configurable and should be replaced by the manufacturer limit in a real installation.

6. GROSS_POWER_DROP — high
Trigger: irradiance >= 300 W/m², expected power >= 25 W, and performance_ratio < 0.50.
Meaning: measured power is less than half of the irradiance- and temperature-adjusted expectation.

7. POSSIBLE_SHADING — warning
Trigger: irradiance >= 400 W/m², expected power >= 25 W, and performance_ratio < 0.75 after higher-priority faults are excluded.
Meaning: output is materially below expectation. The demo labels this possible shading because a single panel sample cannot prove shade without peer, spatial, or time-series comparison.

8. LOW_VOLTAGE — warning
Trigger: irradiance >= 200 W/m² and voltage is greater than 1.5 V but below 20 V.
Meaning: panel voltage is below its expected operating range under useful sunlight.

9. NORMAL
Meaning: telemetry is consistent with the simulated irradiance- and temperature-adjusted operating envelope.

Interpretation rules
- Temperature and irradiance are telemetry measurements, not fault categories by themselves.
- LOW_IRRADIANCE is an environmental state and should not be described as equipment failure.
- Use expected_power and performance_ratio when explaining power loss.
- Do not diagnose snapped diodes, inverter-offline, string-offline, degradation, soiling, or confirmed shading from the current demo because those require additional sensors, peer comparison, heartbeat history, or specialized models.
- When evidence is insufficient, say what is known from telemetry and what additional evidence is needed.
